## 1. The headline finding

**The two categories are disjoint. Nobody spans both.**

| Category | Players | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| **Wedding website / RSVP** | Vowly, Nuptl, RSVPMePls, Bridalpod, My Wedding Planner, InviteCount, Loved Concepts, WedPlanner | The *before* — RSVP, guest list, budget, seating, e-invites |
| **Live guest photo wall** | PhotoShare.ph (₱999), EventPix.ph (₱1,299), Guestpix, Kululu, Fotify, Wedibox, LiveWall | The *during* — QR upload to a reception screen |

Not one wedding-website competitor offers a live reception wall. Not one photo-wall competitor offers a
wedding website with RSVP. **Reelday is the only product found that spans both** — and adds trivia,
reactions, video greetings, and the SDE recap reel that nothing in either set has.

**Strategic read:** the free wedding website is a commodity with 8+ credible PH players. The
website+wall combination has zero direct competitors. The moat is the span, not either half.

## 4. Threat ranking

**1. Vowly — the serious one.** Only competitor with the full localization stack (GCash, entourage,
Tagalog, Viber/Messenger) *plus* scale claims (5,000+ couples) *plus* an AI feature story. Its ₱2,499/event
Complete plan sits directly on Reelday's paid price band. Reelday already has
[alternatives-vowly.html](frontend/alternatives-vowly.html) — that page is correctly prioritized.

**2. Nuptl — the closest philosophical match.** A 2-person Manila team, ₱999 lifetime, and the most
authentic PH-specific framing found anywhere ("ninong & ninang," abuloy envelopes, rainy-season planning,
"not a venture-funded chatbot"). 36 published articles = a real content engine, which is exactly the
SEO asset Reelday lacks. Lifetime ₱999 also undercuts Reelday's paid tiers on perceived value.

**3. InviteCount — the feature-list twin.** Peso pricing, GCash/Maya/QR gift funds, principal-sponsor
entourage, Messenger RSVP, free ≤50 guests. On paper this is Reelday's localization pitch verbatim.
**Blocked by 403 on two fetch attempts — needs manual review.**

**4-7. Bridalpod, My Wedding Planner, RSVPMePls, Loved Concepts.** Real but weaker. RSVPMePls claims
"Built for Philippine weddings" while showing **zero** actual PH-specific features — a positioning
claim without substance, and a page Reelday can beat on merit.

## 5. Gaps and opportunities

**Confirmed white space:**
- **Event-day layer.** Nobody in the wedding-website category has anything that happens *at* the wedding.
- **SDE recap reel.** No competitor in either category offers it.
- **Interactive program** (trivia, live reactions, video greetings) — confirms the 2026-06-11 finding.
- **Genuine build-before-signup.** Only Nuptl comes close, and only partially.

**Closed space — stop claiming these as differentiators:**
- PH localization alone. Vowly, Nuptl, and InviteCount all have it.
- Free wedding website. Six of seven offer one.
- GCash. Vowly and InviteCount both have it.
- Entourage/ninong-ninang. Vowly, Nuptl, and InviteCount all have it.

**Content gap — the real ranking obstacle:** Nuptl has 36 articles, Vowly has a blog ranking for
"best wedding website builder RSVP tracking philippines 2026," and PH wedding blogs
(Bride and Breakfast, EventNest, Storia) own the roundup slots. Reelday has **three** SEO pages and
no blog. This — not on-page schema — is why `free wedding website philippines` is out of reach today.

---

## 6. Recommended repositioning

Reelday's landing page currently competes in the commodity half of the market on the commodity
attributes. The evidence says lead with the span instead:

> **The only Filipino wedding website that turns into a live photo wall at your reception.**

Free site with RSVP, countdown, entourage, and find-your-seat like everyone else — then on the day,
the same QR puts every guest's photos on the reception screen, with trivia, video greetings, and a
same-day-edit recap reel. **No competitor in this research can match the second sentence.**
