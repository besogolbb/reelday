## Positioning & Messaging

Sells **editable Canva templates**, not a hosted product. Couples buy a template, customize it in Canva,
and publish on Canva's free hosting. Emphasis on unlimited edits with no expiration and no coding.

---

## Product & Features

Editable templates (fonts, colors, text, photos) · no coding · password-protected sites · free hosting
and domain via Canva · personalized couple-name URLs · QR code RSVP · unlimited edits, no expiry ·
mobile compatible · countdown timers, music playlists, maps, video embeds.

**PH specifics:** peso pricing and a PH base — but no functional localization (no GCash, entourage,
Tagalog, or Messenger).

**Absent:** real RSVP database/tracking · seating · live wall · SDE · anything event-day.

---

## Strengths

- Genuinely different model — one-time purchase with no subscription and no platform lock-in
- "Unlimited edits, never expires" answers a real fear about SaaS wedding tools shutting down
- Sells a broader creative catalogue (dance intro videos, moodboards) — multiple revenue lines
- Five substantive testimonials, the most detailed social proof in the set

## Weaknesses

- No free tier — pure paid, so it doesn't compete for "free wedding website" traffic
- Canva dependency caps the product ceiling; RSVP is a QR link, not real tracking
- No guest data, no analytics, no automation
- No PH-functional features despite being PH-based

---

## Competitive Implications for Reelday

**Stronger than us:** none functionally. Better testimonial depth.

**Stronger than them:** everything product — real RSVP tracking, guest data, seating, localization, event day.

**Opportunity:** their customers are design-motivated couples who chose a template because free builders
looked generic. That's a **template/theme quality signal** for Reelday, not a competitive threat. If
Reelday's `/e/<slug>` themes look cheap next to these, that's the real lesson.

**Threat:** minimal. Different model, different buyer, no overlap on free-tier traffic.
