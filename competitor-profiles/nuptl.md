## Positioning & Messaging

**Value prop:** a wedding planner built for Filipino-specific realities rather than "generic Pinterest
workflows" — ninong & ninang tracking, abuloy envelopes, rainy-season planning, Messenger RSVPs.

**Angle:** authentic underdog. "Built by a 2-person team in Manila — not a venture-funded chatbot" is a
direct swipe at Vowly's AI positioning, and it is the sharpest piece of copy in the whole category.

---

## Product & Features

**Free:** 50 guests w/ RSVP, 20 budget categories, public RSVP page, e-invite builder with gallery
templates, basic vendor manager, full article library.

**Premium (₱999 lifetime):** unlimited guests and budget categories, drag-and-drop seating with conflict
detection, budget-vs-actual analytics, custom tasks, gated RSVP with single-use codes, supplier
document vault, priority support, all future features.

**PH localization — deepest found:**
- Ninong & ninang principal-sponsor list with confirmation, gift, and seating management
- Abuloy envelope tracking
- Church + civil dual-ceremony logistics
- Rainy-season planning guidance
- Messenger-native RSVP coordination

**Signup:** no account needed before building; guests RSVP with no login. Hosts sign up to save —
"30 seconds. No credit card." **This is the closest thing to Reelday's build-before-signup flow.**

**Absent:** live photo wall · SDE reel · trivia/polls · live reactions · video greetings · photo gallery.

---

## Strengths

- **₱999 lifetime is aggressive** — undercuts Vowly by 60% and beats any recurring model on perceived value
- Deepest, most credible PH localization in the category (abuloy and rainy-season are details only a local team ships)
- **36 published articles** — a real content engine and the SEO asset Reelday most conspicuously lacks
- Authentic small-team story that resonates in this market
- Near-parity on the no-signup-to-start flow Reelday was planning to claim as differentiating

## Weaknesses

- Planning-tool-first, weakest actual *website* product in the set — e-invite builder, not a full microsite
- No photo gallery at all
- No social proof: no customer counts, no testimonials
- 50-guest free cap, same as Vowly
- No event-day layer

---

## Competitive Implications for Reelday

**Stronger than us:** content volume, localization depth (abuloy, dual-ceremony), lifetime pricing story.

**Stronger than them:** the actual wedding *website* (they ship an e-invite), photo handling, and the
entire event-day layer.

**Opportunity:** they are a planning tool wearing a website's clothes. Reelday's `/e/<slug>` microsite —
countdown, love story, find-your-seat, gallery — is a materially better *website*. An alternatives page
should compete on "a real shareable site, not just an e-invite."

**Threat — the important one:** Nuptl proves a 2-person PH team can out-content Reelday. Their 36
articles are why they surface in AI answers and Reelday does not. **This is the clearest evidence that
the ranking gap is a content-and-authority gap, not an on-page gap.**
