## Positioning & Messaging

**Value prop:** "smart, stress-free & value-driven" planning via integrated budgeting, vendor discovery,
guest management, and digital coordination.

**Angle:** all-in-one planning suite with a vendor marketplace. The website is one module among many,
not the product — closer to a marketplace play than a website builder.

auto-generated seating.

**Signup:** required before accessing tools.

## Strengths

- **Vendor marketplace + virtual expo** is a real moat and a revenue model nobody else in the set has
- Auto-generated seating is a genuine convenience feature
- QR code guest access shows some overlap in thinking with Reelday's QR flow
- Supplier relationships are exactly the distribution channel `docs/MARKETING-PLAN.md` identifies as Reelday's durable moat — they got there first

## Weaknesses

- No pricing transparency whatsoever
- No PH-functional localization despite PH-first branding
- Website is a secondary module — thin against dedicated builders
- Signup gate before building
- No event-day layer

---

## Competitive Implications for Reelday

**Stronger than us:** supplier/vendor network and the virtual expo — a distribution asset, not a feature.

**Stronger than them:** website depth, localization, pricing clarity, event-day layer.

**Opportunity:** not primarily a competitor — **a potential distribution partner.** Their vendor
marketplace is full of the coordinators and venues Reelday's supplier-led strategy targets. Worth
evaluating as a channel before treating them as a rival.

**Threat:** their supplier network compounds. If they bolt on an event-day feature, they'd have both
distribution and product. Low probability near-term; high impact.
