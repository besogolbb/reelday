## Positioning & Messaging

**Value prop:** consolidates "6+ wedding planning apps" into one dashboard — replaces spreadsheets,
group chats, and vendor coordination.

**Angle:** all-in-one + AI. Six AI assistants (invitation writer, seating optimizer, budget advisor,
speech writer, drinks calculator, timeline generator) carry most of the differentiation story.

**Themes:** stress reduction · consolidation · AI automation · Filipino tradition ("kasal") ·
zero commission on gift payments.

---

## Product & Features

**14 tools:** guest management, response tracking, AI seating planner, checklist, vendor management,
wedding page builder, photo gallery, guestbook, gift registry, smart reminders.

**PH localization — complete:**
- GCash, bank transfer, Stripe
- Entourage (ninongs/ninangs) management
- Tagalog invitation generation (2 languages, 6 themes)
- Viber, Messenger, SMS sharing

**Signup:** required for hosts. Guests need none — "everything through one link."

**Absent:** live photo wall · same-day-edit reel · trivia/polls · live reactions · video greetings.
The photo gallery and guestbook are static, post-hoc artifacts, not an event-day experience.

## Strengths

- **Only competitor with the complete localization stack** — GCash + entourage + Tagalog + Viber/Messenger
- Strongest social proof in the category (5,000+ couples, 4 testimonials) — self-reported, unverified
- AI story gives it a modern, differentiated narrative competitors lack
- Publishes SEO content — ranks for "best wedding website builder RSVP tracking philippines 2026"
- Planner Pro tier opens a B2B coordinator channel Reelday has no answer to

## Weaknesses

- ₱2,499/event is a real price wall against Reelday's free tier
- Free tier is tight (50 guests / 50 photos / 200MB) — most PH weddings exceed 50 guests immediately
- Heavy discount framing ("38% off," "44% off") can read as low-confidence pricing
- Signup gate before any building
- **No event-day layer at all**

---

## Competitive Implications for Reelday

**Stronger than us:** localization parity, content/SEO engine, social proof, coordinator channel.

**Stronger than them:** the entire event day. Live wall, trivia, live reactions, video greetings, SDE
recap. Also a genuinely more generous free tier and a build-before-signup flow.

**Opportunity:** their 50-guest free cap is the wedge. A PH wedding averages far more, so every Vowly
free user hits the wall fast. Reelday's free site with no such cap is a direct answer — and the
[alternatives-vowly.html](../frontend/alternatives-vowly.html) page should lead with that plus the
event-day gap.

**Threat:** if Vowly ships any live-wall feature, Reelday's only uncontested ground narrows sharply.
Worth monitoring their changelog quarterly.
