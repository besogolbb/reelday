Frequently asked questions

Is Reelday's wedding website really free?

Yes. The wedding website — countdown, love story, RSVP, details, entourage, and find-your-seat — is free forever on Reelday's Tala plan, no credit card required. You only pay if you later want the live guest photo/video wall for the reception itself.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The builder is a guided, tab-by-tab wizard — you fill in your names, date, venue, and story, and the site is generated automatically with a live preview.

Is it in Tagalog or English?

Both. Reelday's event websites support an English/Tagalog toggle for guest-facing labels.

Can guests find their table on the website?

Yes — the Find Your Seat section lets each guest type their name and see only their own table assignment, so the full guest list is never publicly exposed.
