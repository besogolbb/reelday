FAQ

Common questions.

Do guests need to download an app? ▾
No. Guests just scan the QR code with their phone camera. The upload page opens in any browser — works on iPhone, Android, kahit lumang phone.
Can I pay with GCash or Maya? ▾
Yes! We accept GCash, Maya, and credit/debit cards via PayMongo. 100% secure and encrypted.
How many photos can guests upload? ▾
Unlimited. All plans include unlimited uploads from all guests — photos and videos included. No per-upload charges ever.
How long after the event can I download photos? ▾
Libre plan: 6 months. Selebrasyon: 12 months. Pro: lifetime. Download anytime within your access window.
What types of events does Reelday support? ▾
Weddings, debuts, birthdays, 7th birthdays, baptisms, reunions, corporate events, and memorials — each with its own cinematic theme and Taglish UI.
Can I moderate what appears on the live wall? ▾
Yes. From your dashboard you can approve or delete any upload before it appears on the wall — full control at all times.
