— Common questions

Mga tanong?

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR, the upload page opens in any browser, and they share. That's the whole point.

What if the venue Wi-Fi is bad?

Guests can upload over mobile data. There's no Wi-Fi dependency — the upload page works on any connection.

Can we screen photos before they go live?

Yes. One-tap moderation from your phone. Approve, hide, or feature. Defaults to auto-approve so you can stay present.

Do we keep the photos forever?

Yes. Download a full-resolution .zip any time from your dashboard. Pro events stay archived for life.

Paano mag-bayad? (How do we pay?)

We accept GCash, Maya, and credit/debit cards via PayMongo. GCash payments are verified within 1 hour.

Pwede ba namin gamitin sa Filipino?

Oo naman. The guest upload page is fully bilingual — guests pick EN or FIL when they open it.
