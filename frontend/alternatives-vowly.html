What to look for in a PH wedding website builder

The basics — free hosting, RSVP tracking, a countdown — are table stakes now. A few PH-focused builders, including Vowly, offer a similar free-website-with-RSVP starting point for Filipino couples — a good sign for the category. The differences that actually matter show up in the details: does the seat-finder expose your whole guest list to anyone who visits, does it handle a formal entourage/principal-sponsors section without a workaround, and does the same product carry any value into the wedding day itself, or does it stop being useful the moment guests RSVP? We haven't done a line-by-line feature audit of every PH builder, so treat the comparison below as what to check for yourself, not a claim about any one competitor's specific limitations.

Frequently asked questions

What makes Reelday different from other Philippine wedding website builders?

Most PH-focused wedding website tools stop at the website. Reelday's free website is the same idea — RSVP, countdown, find-your-seat — but it connects to an optional paid upgrade: a live guest photo and video wall that runs on a screen during the reception itself, something the website-only tools don't offer.

Is a Philippine-focused wedding website builder better than a US one?

For a wedding actually happening in the Philippines, yes — a PH-built tool understands Tagalog labeling, GCash payment, entourage conventions, and local venue norms that US-first tools like Zola or The Knot don't account for.
