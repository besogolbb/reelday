This Privacy Policy explains how Reelday Inc. collects, uses, and protects your personal information in compliance with the Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

1. Who We Are

Reelday Inc. is the personal information controller for data collected through reelday.ph. Our Data Protection Officer can be reached at admin@reelday.ph.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Host Account Data

  • Registration: full name, email address, phone number, password (stored as a one-way hash).
  • Event data: couple/celebrant names, event type, event date, selected plan.
  • Payment data: transaction reference numbers and payment status. We do not store card numbers, CVVs, or GCash PINs — these are handled exclusively by PayMongo.

2.2 Guest Upload Data

Guests upload photos and videos without creating an account. We do not collect guest names or email addresses. To operate the live wall we generate a random per-browser device identifier so that a single guest's uploads can be grouped together and so the wall can show real-time presence (who is actively viewing). This identifier is not linked to your real identity and is stored only for the lifetime of the event's gallery — it is permanently deleted together with the media when the gallery expires. IP addresses are used transiently for abuse prevention and are not stored permanently.

2.3 Technical Data

  • Server logs: request timestamps, IP addresses, HTTP status codes. Retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging purposes.
  • Browser local storage: used client-side to preserve session state (e.g., guest upload tokens). No tracking cookies are used for advertising.
