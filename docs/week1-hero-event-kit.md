# Week 1 — Hero-Event Sourcing Kit

The goal of the free events is **footage + testimonials**, not charity. You
only have capacity for **1–3**, so this kit is built to *attract good events
and filter out freebie-seekers* — and to convert everyone you don't pick into
a paying Founding 20 booking.

> Strategy in one line: frame it as a **selective "Founding Events" feature**
> (we're choosing a few), not a giveaway (free for all). Selectivity + a clear
> exchange (testimonial + footage) is what repels the "ako po, libre ba talaga?"
> crowd and attracts hosts who'll actually show up and let you film.

---

## A. The post — your OWN Facebook Page (promotional is fine here)

> 📸 **Looking for 3 "Founding Events" this June**
>
> We're new, and we're choosing **3 celebrations** happening this June to feature with **Reelday Dalisay — free (worth ₱2,990).**
>
> Reelday is a **live photo wall**: your guests scan a QR, and their photos & videos appear **live on your venue screen** during the event. Guests' phones become your second photographer. No app, no login, no hassle.
>
> 💛 **You get:** the full Dalisay experience free — live wall, unlimited guest uploads, event website, and a Same-Day-Edit recap reel.
> 🎥 **In return:** we capture a few photos/short clips of the wall in action, and you give us a short testimonial after.
>
> **To qualify:** your event is in **June 2026**, and your venue has a **TV/projector/screen** we can display the wall on.
>
> 👉 Interested? Comment **"ME"** or send us your **event date** in a message. We'll reply with a few quick questions and pick our 3. 🤍

*Why it works:* leads with scarcity (3) + the outcome (guests = photographers), names the value (₱2,990 free), states the exchange plainly (filters freebie-seekers), and gives ONE action.

---

## B. The post / comment — INSIDE Facebook wedding & event groups (soft, approval-safe)

Groups auto-reject ads. Don't paste Version A here. Lead with value + a question, keep the brand low-key, and take it to DM. Post only where rules allow, or drop it as a *helpful reply* on a "looking for suppliers / ideas" thread.

> Question for the **June couples** here 🙂 — would a **live photo wall** at your reception be something you'd want? (Guests' phone photos & videos show up on the venue screen in real time — basically turns every guest into a second photographer.)
>
> We're a small PH startup setting up **a few June events free** to gather real feedback and footage before our wider launch. If that sounds useful, comment or message me and I'll share how it works. No pressure 🤍

*Why it works:* it's a question, not a pitch; "a few, free, for feedback" reads as genuine early-stage outreach, not spam; the ask is soft ("I'll share how it works") so the bot/admin is less likely to flag it.

> ⚠️ **Group rules first.** Read each group's pinned rules. Many require supplier posts on specific days or ban them entirely. When in doubt, *reply helpfully on other people's threads* instead of making your own post — that's how you build presence without getting removed.

## D. The qualifying questions (send in DM after they raise their hand)

Keep it to these 6. They double as your filter — anyone who won't answer #5 or #6 is not a hero event.

1. **What's the event and the exact date?** *(Must be in June, and a date you're free to support.)*
2. **Where's the venue?** *(Is there a TV, projector, or big screen we can show the wall on? No screen = no wall.)*
3. **Roughly how many guests?** *(More guests = more uploads = better footage. ~50+ is ideal.)*
4. **Is there reliable WiFi at the venue, or can you provide a mobile hotspot?** *(The wall needs internet.)*
5. **Are you okay with us capturing a few photos / short clips of the wall during the event, and giving a short testimonial after?** ← **the key filter.** A hesitant "maybe" here = pass.
6. **Who's the main contact / is there a coordinator we'd coordinate with?**

> Optional bonus: ask for the venue name and the couple's IG — a styled, photogenic venue makes far better promo footage than a plain function hall.

---

## E. How to pick your 1–3 (selection rubric)

Score each applicant 1–3 on these, pick the top scorers:

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Date works for you** | You must be able to set up + capture that day. Non-negotiable. |
| **Screen at venue** | No display = the wall's whole "wow" is invisible. Non-negotiable. |
| **Photogenic / visible venue** | Styled wedding or nice debut > plain hall. Drives better promo clips. |
| **Guest count (~50+)** | More guests = more uploads = a fuller, more impressive wall on camera. |
| **Testimonial-willing & responsive** | They answered #5 with an enthusiastic yes and reply quickly. |
| **Variety** | If you take 3, mix event types (e.g. 1 wedding + 1 debut) for varied content. |

> Don't take the first 3 who reply. Collect applicants for ~3–4 days, then choose. Quality of footage in June decides how well July sells.

## F. Turn everyone you DIDN'T pick into a paying Founding 20 lead

This is the whole reason the post earns its keep — most applicants won't be chosen, and they're warm.

> Thanks so much for your interest, [name]! 🙏 We had more amazing events apply than our 3 free slots, so we couldn't include yours this round — but I'd love to still make your [event] special.
>
> We're offering our first **20 events 50% off** as Founding bookings: **Dalisay for ₱1,490** (normally ₱2,990), with a money-back guarantee if your guests don't fill the wall. Want me to send your discount link? 💛

*(That link is your `FOUNDING20` checkout link from the admin Coupons tab.)*

---

## G. Operating notes

- **Cap the free offer at 3.** If the post blows up, that's *good* — funnel the overflow to Founding 20 (Section F). Don't over-promise free events you can't staff.
- **Set expectations with the 3 you pick:** confirm you'll do a dry run before guests arrive, that one person should operate the wall, and use Microsoft Edge on the wall laptop (per the booking-confirm email guidance).
- **Lock the testimonial + footage permission in writing** (even just a DM "yes, confirmed") *before* the event, not after.
- **Log every applicant** in your lead sheet (name · event date · channel · picked? · follow-up sent?) — the not-picked list is your first batch of Founding 20 outreach.
