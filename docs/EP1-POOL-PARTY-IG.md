end cards appearing early.

## 3. Text + safe zones (CapCut)

- Keep ALL text inside the **middle 60% of the frame** — IG UI eats the top
  ~80px and bottom ~180px; CapCut's default title position is safe, just never
  drag text near the bottom caption zone.
- Style: bold white, thin black outline or subtle shadow. One line at a time,
  max ~6 words. No fancy fonts — readable at arm's length on a phone.
- Brand color pop allowed on the payoff + end card: `#c45a3a`.

## 4. Sound (this is where IG reach comes from)

1. In CapCut: keep the **natural party audio** (kids screaming = the hook),
   level it so it doesn't clip.
2. Export WITHOUT music.
3. In the **Instagram app**, add a **trending audio** (browse Reels → save 2–3
   trending PH sounds earlier in the week) at **low volume (~20%)** under the
   natural sound. Trending-audio reels get distribution that baked-in music
   doesn't.

## 5. Export + cover

- 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, highest quality.
- **Cover frame:** the peak of Shot A with `Totoong party 'to` readable —

## 7. Posting protocol (Saturday)

1. Post **7–9 PM PHT** from the IG app (audio added there).
2. **First hour is the algorithm window:** reply to every comment (anyone who
   comments PARTY gets the demo link + offer in DM within minutes), share the

   extra effort, per plan — TikTok is a re-post bin).
