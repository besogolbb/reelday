## 0. The one-paragraph thesis

Reelday is **not** a content product you sell with reach — it's an **event utility** you sell to people who have an event in the next 1–3 months. At any given moment, 99% of people who watch a wedding reel are *not* planning a wedding, so views without buyers is the *expected* result of broad content, not a failure of the videos. The fastest path to paying customers in 32 days is to **stop chasing reach and start reaching intent**: people actively planning a wedding/debut/party right now (FB planning groups, recently-engaged announcements, vendor referrals) — plus a **time-boxed founding offer** that converts the Filipino "pa-libre?" reflex into a paid booking with proof attached. Organic content's only job this month is to make the brand *look legit* when an intent-lead checks the profile.

| Symptom | Real cause | Fix |

**Takeaway:** the videos aren't the problem. The *targeting* and the *call-to-action / offer* are. Pivot from "go viral" to "reach the ~5,000 PH couples/parents with an event in the next 90 days."

## 2. The funnel, ranked by intent (where the ₱ actually comes from)

1. **FB event-planning groups** — wedding, debut, kids' party, christening, corporate/HR event groups. People literally post "looking for…". *Highest intent, ₱0.* → helpful answers + DM.
2. **Vendor referrals** — coordinators, photographers, videographers, venues, host/emcee. They already own the client relationship and the event date. One coordinator = many events. → partner offer (free Hiraya trial / referral cut).
3. **Recently-engaged / debut announcements** — searchable + visible on FB/IG. Direct, warm DM outreach.
4. **Paid lead ads (₱5k)** — FB/IG lead form targeted at engaged + event interests, retargeting Page/profile visitors. Only after we have 1 hero testimonial to anchor the ad.
5. **Organic content (TikTok/IG/FB reels)** — *trust layer only.* Makes the profile look real when an intent-lead checks you out. Not the buyer source this month.

> Rule of thumb for June: **80% of effort on tiers 1–3 (intent), 20% on tiers 4–5.** Content feels productive but converts last.

## 3. The Founding Offer (the conversion lever)

Brand-new product + no reviews = buyers hesitate. Beat hesitation with scarcity + risk-reversal + proof-trade:

> **"Reelday Founding 20"** — the first 20 events booked before **June 30** get:
> - **Dalisay for ₱1,490** (50% off ₱2,990) — full features + event website (SDE is hidden/not offered yet — keep it out of all offer copy)
> - **Money-back guarantee**: if your guests don't fill the wall, full refund, no questions
> - In exchange: a short testimonial + permission to use 2–3 clips/photos from your event
> - Founding badge / locked-in price on any future event

Why this works in the PH market:
- **Anchored discount** (₱2,990 → ₱1,490) reads as real value, not "cheap."
- **Guarantee** removes the "what if it flops" fear on an unproven product.
- **Scarcity (20 + deadline)** creates urgency — the single biggest driver of "book now" vs "maybe later."
- **Proof-trade** turns each paying customer into your next ad. Compounding.
- Keep **Sinag ₱1,490** as the entry point for debuts/birthdays/parties (smaller events) — same price as the Founding Dalisay, so you can steer by event type.

Free events (your 1–3 capacity) are **invite-only hero shoots**, never advertised — handpick high-visibility events (a styled wedding, a photogenic debut) for the best footage.

## 7. Content system (the trust layer — ship, don't agonize)

- **Cadence:** 1 post/day per platform max, peak 7–9 PM PHT. Consistency > volume.
- **Every post needs ONE CTA.** For conversion-intent posts: "Comment WALL or DM for a free demo." For reach posts: a save/share trigger ("tag someone planning a wedding").
- **Hook first second** (esp. TikTok): show the *payoff* frame — wall full of guest photos on a big screen — before any text.
- **6 content pillars:** ① Wow moment ② How it works ③ Social proof/testimonial ④ Before vs after ⑤ Behind the event ⑥ Seasonal/trend. In June, weight toward ③ and ④ (they convert).
- **Production:** reuse the existing Remotion pipeline — `tools/wall-recorder/MARKETING.md` (platform matrix, 3 templates: FB 16:9, IG 9:16, TikTok 9:16) + compositions in `tools/wall-recorder/src/Root.tsx`. Don't build new tooling; feed hero footage into the existing templates.
- **Safe zones:** nothing below 80% from top; FB UI covers bottom ~15%, TikTok ~20%.
- **Never:** lead with features, post pricing before proof, buy fake engagement, delete posts in first 7 days.
