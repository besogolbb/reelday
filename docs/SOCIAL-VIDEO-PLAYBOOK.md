# Reelday Social Video Playbook

> Reusable rules for every marketing Reel/TikTok/FB video.
> Born from the post-mortem of the first wedding film (`scan.mp4`, Jun 2026):
> 203 viewers, **31% avg watched, drop-off at 0:04, 1 like, 0 comments, 0 shares.**
> The footage was good. The *structure* killed it. This doc exists so we never
> repeat those mistakes.

---

## The one lesson that explains the failure

**We front-loaded setup and back-loaded the magic.**

The three best moments — the laughing selfie (0:13), Lola's reaction (0:22), and
faces-on-the-live-wall (0:28) — all landed *after* 70% of viewers had already left.
The video opened on a man quietly looking at his phone, and at the exact 0:04
drop-off point he was *frowning in confusion*. On silent autoplay, with no on-screen
text, viewers had no reason to stay.

Every rule below flows from this one lesson.

---

## RULE 1 — Hook in the first 2 seconds with the PAYOFF, not the setup

The opening frame must be the **most emotional / most surprising** moment in the
whole video — usually a face reacting with joy.

- ✅ Open on: Tita shrieking "tayo 'yun!", Lola lighting up, a crowd cheering at the wall
- ❌ Never open on: someone scanning a QR, looking at a phone, walking, or any setup

**Show the result first. Explain how second.** People stay for the emotion, then get
curious about the tool. Explaining a tool to people who don't yet care = instant scroll.

---

## RULE 2 — Burn in on-screen text from frame 1

Most FB/IG/TikTok views are **muted**. If the hook lives in the audio (a shout, a
line of dialogue), silent viewers get nothing. The text carries the hook.

- Big, bold caption on screen by 0:00 — readable in 1 second
- Taglish, emotional, ideally a question or a "pag..." setup
  - e.g. **"Pag lumabas ka sa wedding screen 🥹"**
- Keep text in the **safe zone** (not behind the FB/TikTok UI at the bottom/right)
- One idea per caption; swap captions every 2–4 seconds to pace the story

---

## RULE 3 — Structure: Hook → Tease → How → Payoff → CTA

A repeatable skeleton for any Reelday video (~12–15s total):

```
0:00–0:02  HOOK     Strongest reaction + big text caption
0:02–0:04  TEASE    The product result (faces on the live wall)
0:04–0:08  HOW      FAST montage: scan → upload → appears (≈1s each)
0:08–0:11  PAYOFF   Second emotional hit (a different person reacting)
0:11–0:14  CTA      On a joy frame: "Gusto mo 'to sa event? Comment WALL 👇"
```

The setup footage (scanning, tapping) still gets used — but as a **fast mid-roll
montage**, never the opening.

---

## RULE 4 — Keep it SHORT (12–15s for cold audiences)

- 30s was too long for an audience bailing at 4s. Shorter = higher % watched = more reach.
- The algorithm rewards completion. A 12s video watched to 80% beats a 30s watched to 31%.
- Make a long cut only for warm/retargeting audiences, never for the cold first post.

---

## RULE 5 — Ask for engagement EARLY and SPECIFICALLY

0 comments = nobody was asked while still watching.

- Put the CTA on-screen by ~0:05–0:08, not only at the end (where <10% reach)
- Use a **comment trigger**: "Comment WALL para sa event mo 👇" — comments boost reach
  AND give warm leads to DM
- One clear action. Don't stack "like + comment + share + visit site."

---

## RULE 6 — Technical QC before posting (non-negotiable checklist)

The first video shipped with a **Veo watermark** (0:22) and a **dimmed/dark** product
shot (0:28). Both look amateur and hurt trust. Always check:

- [ ] **No AI watermarks** (Veo/Flow/etc.) — crop, cover, or regenerate the frame
- [ ] **Brightness** — the product/wall shots must be bright and clear, not dimmed
- [ ] **No blurry filler frames** — cut weak transition frames
- [ ] **Text in safe zone** — not hidden behind platform UI
- [ ] **First frame (thumbnail)** is a strong, bright, emotional face
- [ ] **Captions/subtitles** on all dialogue (for muted viewers)
- [ ] **Vertical 9:16**, 1080×1920 export
- [ ] **Loud, clean audio** if sound matters; never rely on audio alone for the hook

---

## RULE 7 — Caption & hashtags (the post itself)

- **Caption** leads with emotion in Taglish, not an English description.
  - ❌ "The reaction of the visitors is not the same when their faces appear..."
  - ✅ "Uy, lumabas ka sa wedding screen?! 🥹 'Yung reaction, priceless."
- End caption with the same comment CTA as the video.
- Hashtags: ~12–15 for IG, ~4–6 for TikTok. Mix broad (#WeddingPH #FilipinoWedding)
  + niche (#LivePhotoWall #EventStylingPH) + brand (#Reelday #ReeldayPH).

---

## RULE 8 — Test, measure, iterate (per post)

Log every post against these and compare:

| Metric | Target (cold audience) | First video |
|---|---|---|
| Avg % watched | **>50%** | 31% ❌ |
| Hook hold (3s view rate) | **>60%** | ~39% ❌ |
| Comments | **>0, ideally CTA-driven** | 0 ❌ |
| Shares | **>0** | 0 ❌ |

- If avg % watched < 50% → the hook/opening is the problem. Re-cut hook-first.
- If watched is high but 0 comments → the CTA is missing or too late.
- Change **one variable per test** (hook, length, or CTA) so you know what moved it.

---

## Pre-publish checklist (copy this for every video)

```
[ ] Opens on the strongest emotional frame (not setup)
[ ] Big Taglish text caption on screen by 0:00
[ ] Total length 12–15s
[ ] Setup footage is a fast mid-roll montage, not the open
[ ] Two emotional payoffs (e.g. Tita + Lola)
[ ] CTA on-screen by ~0:06 ("Comment WALL 👇")
[ ] No AI watermark anywhere
[ ] Product/wall shots are bright, not dimmed
[ ] No blurry filler frames
[ ] Subtitles on all dialogue
[ ] Caption leads with Taglish emotion + comment CTA
[ ] 12–15 hashtags (IG) / 4–6 (TikTok)
```
