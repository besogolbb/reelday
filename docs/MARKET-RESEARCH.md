# PH Market Research & Go/No-Go Verdict
**Researched 2026-06-11 · feeds MARKETING-PLAN.md · sources at bottom**

## Verdict: **GO — conditionally.**

Demand is real, unit economics are exceptional, and the interactive layer is
uncontested locally. But the plain photo-wall layer is already commoditized at
₱999–free, so Reelday lives or dies on (a) selling the *interactive program*,
never the photo wall, and (b) hitting the Jul 31 kill-criteria below. This is
a distribution problem, not a product or market problem.

---

## 1. Demand volume (PSA data)

| Event type | Volume / year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Weddings | **371,825** (2024) | –10.2% vs 2023 (414k) — declining but massive. **CALABARZON is the #1 region: 54,981 (14.8%)** — Reelday's home turf. NCR 48k, C. Luzon 42k. Peaks: Feb, Jun, Dec |
| Live births | **~1.45M** (2023) | Proxy for christenings + 1st birthdays ≈ ~2–2.5M kid-celebration events/yr |
| Debuts, graduations, corporate | large, unquantified | upside, not the base case |

Addressable celebrations: conservatively **2M+/year** with real budgets. Even
0.01% penetration = 200 events/yr.

## 2. Willingness to pay (what hosts already spend)

| Item | Range | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Wedding (2025 typical) | ₱300k–1M+ ("affordable" ₱50–300k) | ₱2,990 is ~0.5–1% of budget |
| 1st birthday (50 pax example) | ~₱61.5k — incl. host ₱8k, entertainment ₱10k, photography ₱3.5k | **₱1,490 is cheaper than every line item it competes with** |
| Christening (200 pax example) | ~₱70k — incl. magician, host, sound | same |
| **Photobooth (closest analog — a default PH party line item)** | **₱1,250–15,000/event** | The market already pays this price band for guest-photo entertainment. Reelday at ₱1,490 enters at the *bottom* of an accepted category |

## 3. Competition

**Local, direct:**
- **PhotoShare.ph — ₱999/event:** photos ONLY, 30-day storage, real-time wall.
  **No video, no reactions, no polls/games, no greetings, no event website.**
  Social proof: one case study (127 photos). Early/thin.
- **EventPix.ph — from ₱1,299/event:** photo + video sharing.

**Global (a Google search away, several with free tiers):** Guestpix
("150k+ hosts, 100 countries" — proves the category worldwide), Kululu,
Fotify, WedUploader, CatchMemo.

**What NOBODY sells locally:** the interactive program — live trivia/polls
with leaderboard + emoji reactions + video greetings + event website in one
QR. That's Reelday's whole differentiation, and it confirms the
MARKETING-PLAN positioning ("interactive party wall", never "photo wall").

**Read on the competition:** their existence is *good news at this stage* —
local players charging ₱999–1,299 prove PH hosts pay for this; their feature
thinness is the gap. The risk is speed: features are copyable, so the durable
moat must be **supplier distribution + local presence + proof footage**, not
the feature list.

## 4. Profitability (from INFRA-COSTS.md, 2026-05-19)

| | |
|---|---|
| Infra cost / event | **₱14 (30-day) – ₱25 (90-day)** |
| Gross margin @ ₱1,490 launch | **~96–99%** (after ~3.5% PayMongo) |
| Break-even on the ₱5k ad budget | **4 events** at launch price |
| At 100 events/mo (90-day retention) | ~₱1,600/mo infra vs ₱149k–299k revenue |

There is no scale at which infra threatens the business (R2 free egress is
the moat). Profitability is purely a **customer-acquisition-cost** question —
which is exactly what the supplier-led plan attacks.

## 5. Honest risk register

1. **Commoditized base layer** — competing on "photo wall" = race to ₱999/free.
   *Mitigation: interactive positioning, already locked.*
2. **Copyable features** — PhotoShare could ship reactions in a month.
   *Mitigation: move fast on supplier lock-in + footage bank.*
3. **Declining marriages** (–10%/yr) — *mitigation: kids'-party niche is the
   bigger, stabler pool anyway (1.45M births vs 372k weddings).*
4. **Founder time** (~8 h/wk) — the real constraint. *Mitigation: supplier
   leverage; pre-committed kill rules so time isn't burned on a dead lane.*
5. **Category education** — "interactive wall" is a new phrase for buyers.
   *Mitigation: sell the moments (game/greeting/wall), demo at real events.*

## 6. Kill criteria (pre-committed — same as MARKETING-PLAN §7)

By **Jul 31**, having actually executed (≥50 supplier DMs, ≥8 episodes
posted, ₱5k ads run):
- **≥3 paid bookings AND ≥2 active supplier partners → continue and scale.**
- Below that → the problem is demand-at-price: run the ₱990 test for 2 weeks.
  Still nothing → shelve SaaS ambitions, keep it as a weekend service
  business (₱2,990 with personal setup = still profitable per event) while
  the job search stays priority.
