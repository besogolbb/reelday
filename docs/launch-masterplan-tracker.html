🎯 The thesis (read once, then act)
Sell to intent, not reach. Reelday is an event utility, not a content product. At any moment ~99% of people watching a wedding reel are NOT planning a wedding — so views with zero conversion is expected, not a content failure. Spend 80% of effort reaching people who already have an upcoming event (FB groups, DMs, vendors) and only 20% on broad content (which exists only to make the brand look legit when a lead checks your profile).

Channel priority1) FB groups + warm DMs (free, highest intent) · 2) Vendor partners (multiplier) · 3) ₱5k paid ads (only after 1 testimonial) · 4) IG (proof + DM funnel) · 5) TikTok (minimal).
Money ruleDon't spend a peso on ads until ≥1 testimonial exists. Then ~₱3k lead ad + ~₱2k boost; reallocate weekly to the lowest cost-per-booking source.
