🧭 The thesis — read once, then act
Sell to intent, not reach. Reelday is an event utility, not a content product. At any moment ~99% of people watching a wedding reel are NOT planning a wedding — so views with zero conversion is expected, not a content failure. Spend 80% of effort reaching people who already have an upcoming event (FB groups, DMs, vendors); only 20% on broad content, whose only job is to make the brand look legit when a lead checks your profile.
🎁 The offer
Founding 20 — first 20 events booked before June 30 get Dalisay ₱1,490 (50% off ₱2,990) + money-back guarantee, in exchange for a testimonial + footage rights. Anchored discount + risk-reversal + scarcity beats "brand-new, no reviews" hesitation.
🎚️ Steer by event
Wedding/milestone → Dalisay (₱1,490) · Debut/birthday/party → Sinag (₱1,490) · Coordinator/venue/multi-event → Hiraya pipeline (free trial now).
📡 Channel order
1) FB groups + warm DMs (free, highest intent) · 2) Vendor partners (multiplier) · 3) ₱5k paid ads (only after 1 testimonial) · 4) IG (proof + DM funnel) · 5) TikTok (minimal).
💰 Money rule
No ad spend until ≥1 testimonial exists. Then ~₱3k lead ad + ~₱2k boost; reallocate weekly to the lowest cost-per-booking source.
