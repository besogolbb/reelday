# Week 1 — Outreach Pack

The system to run your two highest-intent, free channels: **FB groups** and
**warm DMs**, plus **vendor partnerships** (the multiplier). Pair this with the
[hero-event kit](week1-hero-event-kit.md) and the
[lead tracker](lead-tracker-template.csv).

> The rule that makes this work: **capture every lead in the sheet, and follow
> up.** Most bookings happen on the 2nd–3rd touch — an untracked lead is a lost
> sale.

---

## 1. The lead-tracking sheet

Import `docs/lead-tracker-template.csv` into Google Sheets (File → Import →
Upload) or Excel. It has example rows showing the three channels — delete them
once you get real leads.

**Columns:** Date Added · Name · Event Type · Event Date · Channel · Source
Detail · Contact · Stage · Hero Candidate? · Founding20 Link Sent? · Last Touch
· Next Follow-up · Notes.

**The pipeline stages (the "Stage" column):**

```
New        → just added, not contacted yet
Contacted  → you sent the first message
Replied    → they responded
Qualified  → confirmed they have a June/soon event (a real buyer)
Sent offer → you sent the Founding 20 link or hero-event form
Booked ✅  → paid (or locked as a free hero event)
Lost       → no event / not interested / went cold after 3 touches
```

**How to work it daily (5 min):** sort by **Next Follow-up**, message anyone due
today, update their Stage + Last Touch, set the next follow-up date. That's your
whole CRM.

## 2. FB group reply playbook

Groups are your #1 free channel — people literally post asking for ideas and
suppliers. But **promo posts get you flagged.** You win by being a helpful
member, then taking interested people to DM.

### Setup (do once)
- Join **15–20 groups**: PH wedding planning, debut, kids' party / christening,
  and **area-specific** ones (e.g. "Cavite Weddings", "Tagaytay Weddings",
  "Laguna Events"), plus budget-wedding and "wedding suppliers" groups.
- **Fix your personal profile first** — when you comment, people click your
  name. Your bio/featured post should make it obvious you're reelday.ph. (This
  is your storefront; a blank profile kills trust.)
- Read each group's **pinned rules.** Many allow supplier posts only on certain
  days or ban them outright. Respect it — a ban kills the channel.

### What to do every day
Answer **2–3 threads** helpfully. Don't post your own ad. Match the thread type:

**Thread: "Any suggestions for [photo/entertainment/unique idea]?"**
> For photos, one tip that's a hit at PH receptions: a **QR live photo wall** —
> guests scan a code, and their phone photos & videos show up on the venue
> screen in real time. Basically every guest becomes a second photographer 📸
> Happy to share how it works if you want 🙂

**Thread: "Looking for affordable suppliers / on a budget"**
> If budget's tight, a smart add-on is a live photo wall instead of a second
> photographer — guests' own phones fill it, so you get tons of candid shots for
> a fraction of the cost. I can send details if helpful 💛

**Thread: a "promote your business" / supplier day (when rules allow)**
> Use **Version A** of the hero-event post (from the hero-event kit), or a short
> intro to reelday.ph + the Founding 20 offer.

**When someone replies "how does it work / interested":**
> Take it to DM. Drop the demo + Founding 20 (see DM scripts below). Log them in
> the sheet as Replied/Qualified.

### Do / Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Comment as a helpful guest | Paste the same ad in 10 groups |
| Take warm replies to DM | Argue or hard-sell in comments |
| Personalize each reply | Copy-paste identical text (bots flag it) |
| Log every interested person | Post promos where rules forbid it |

## 3. Warm DM scripts (couples / debut parents)

Find recently-engaged couples and June debut/party hosts (engagement posts,
"save the date", area wedding groups). Send **10/day.** Personalize the brackets.

**Touch 1 — opener:**
> Hi [name]! Saw your [wedding/debut] is coming up — congrats 🎉 I run
> **reelday.ph**, a live photo wall for PH celebrations: guests scan a QR and
> their photos/videos appear on your venue screen instantly, no app. We're
> onboarding **20 founding events at 50% off (₱1,490, money-back guarantee).**
> Want a free demo link to see it in 30 seconds?

**Touch 2 — if no reply after ~2 days:**
> Hi [name], just following up 🙂 Here's a quick look at the live wall in action:
> [demo link]. We've got [X] of 20 founding slots left at ₱1,490 if you'd like
> to lock yours for [event date].

**Touch 3 — final (after ~2 more days), then mark Lost if no reply:**
> No worries if it's not for you, [name]! If you change your mind before June
> 30, the founding price is here: [FOUNDING20 link]. Wishing you a beautiful
> [event] either way 💛

> Reply within the hour during 7–10 PM PHT — speed-to-lead is the biggest
> conversion factor.

## 4. Vendor partnerships (the multiplier)

One coordinator or photographer owns *many* couples with set event dates. Land a
few partners and they feed you bookings on autopilot.

### Who to target
- **Wedding/event coordinators & planners** ← highest value (they pick suppliers)
- **Photographers & videographers** (complementary — Reelday = extra deliverable, zero extra shooting)
- **Hosts / emcees** (they're on stage and can plug the wall + polls)
- **Venues & event spaces** (they recommend a "house" supplier list)
- **Lights & sounds / AV teams** (they already run the venue screen)
- **Event stylists & photo-booth operators** (adjacent audiences)

### Where to find them
- FB **"wedding suppliers"** groups (Manila, Cebu, Davao, regional)
- IG hashtags: `#manilaweddingphotographer` `#tagaytayweddingcoordinator`
  `#cebuweddingsupplier` `#phdebut` etc.
- Google Maps: "event venue / coordinator near [city]"
- Ask your hero-event couples who their coordinator/photographer is

### The partner offer
> **Free Hiraya account** (₱9,990/yr value) for their next event to try it +
> a **referral cut** for every couple they send who books (e.g. ₱500/event or
> 15%). It makes *their* events look more premium with zero extra work.

### The DM sequence

**Touch 1 — lead with what's in it for THEM:**
> Hi [name]! I build **reelday.ph** — a live guest photo wall + Same-Day-Edit
> recap for events. It makes your events look more premium and gives your
> couples an extra "wow" with **zero extra shooting for you.** I'd love to set
> you up with a **free Hiraya account** for your next event + a **referral cut**
> for couples you send our way. Open to a quick chat?

**Touch 2 — follow-up (~3 days), add proof if you have it:**
> Hi [name], following up 🙂 Here's the wall in action: [demo/hero clip]. Lots of
> PH coordinators are using it as a free value-add for clients. Happy to comp
> your next event so you can see it live — interested?

**Touch 3 — final:**
> All good if the timing's off, [name]! The free-account offer stands whenever
> you've got an event coming up. Here's my link to keep handy: reelday.ph 🤍

**When they say yes — the "how it works" reply:**
> Awesome! Here's how partners run it: (1) I set you up with a free Hiraya
> account, (2) you use it on your next event, (3) for any couple you refer who
> books, you get [₱500 / 15%] per event. I'll send your account + a simple
> referral link so bookings are tracked to you. What's the email for your
> account? 💛

> Log every vendor in the sheet (Channel = Vendor). Even a "not now" is worth a
> follow-up next month — vendors warm up slowly but pay off for a long time.

## 5. Daily rhythm (ties it together, ~45 min)
- **Midday (30m):** 10 warm DMs + answer 2–3 group threads + 1 vendor touch.
- **Anytime (10m):** update the sheet — log new leads, advance stages, set
  next follow-ups.
- **Evening peak (7–10 PM):** reply to everything within the hour.
