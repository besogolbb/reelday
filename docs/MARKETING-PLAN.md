## 0. The five bets (read this when lost)

Everything below follows from five decisions. If a task doesn't serve one of
these, don't do it.

1. **One niche: kids' parties & christenings.** Not weddings, not corporate,
   not "all celebrations." Weddings return when we have a wedding hero event.
2. **One territory: Laguna + South Luzon first.** Win the province before the
   country. Local means you can show up, and showing up is your only unfair
   advantage right now.
3. **Suppliers are the sales force, not social media.** Party hosts, emcees,
   magicians, sound-and-lights, stylists — they're at 2–8 parties a month and
   they run the program. One host partner = a deal pipeline. One viral reel = a
   dopamine hit.
4. **Every event is the ad.** 80 guests watching the wall = 80 warm prospects
   (titas, ninangs, moms with a binyag next month). The job is to harvest that
   room, not to interrupt strangers on a feed.
5. **One offer, one deadline:** Dalisay at **₱1,490 launch price** (₱2,990
   after July 31) + the **Busog Guarantee** — kung hindi napuno ng guests ang
   wall, full refund. No tiers in the pitch, no menu, no thinking required.

### Why I junked the old plan

The June masterplan's instinct (intent > reach) was right, but it sprayed
across every event type nationwide, made the founder the only salesperson, and
hinged on a June 30 deadline that's 19 days away with zero pipeline. This plan
narrows to the market we have proof in (a real kids' pool party), the territory
we can physically serve, and the channel that compounds (suppliers) — on a
7-week runway that's actually winnable evenings-and-weekends.

## 1. Positioning (decided — use verbatim)

**Category:** the **interactive party wall** — never "photo wall."

**The pitch, one breath (Taglish):**
> Hindi lang photo wall. Buong party program 'to: photos ng guests lumalabas
> live sa screen, may emoji reactions, may **trivia game tungkol sa celebrant
> na sinasagot ng guests sa phones nila** — may leaderboard, may winner — at
> video greetings mula sa mga hindi nakapunta. Isang QR lang. Walang app,
> walang login, kahit si Lola kaya.

**The three moments we sell (never sell features, sell these):**

| Moment | Line | Who it hooks |
|---|---|---|
| 🏆 **The game** | "Parlor games, pero digital — tanong sa big screen, sagot sa phone, may fireworks pag may nanalo" | Parents & hosts — it upgrades a ritual every PH party already has |
| 🥹 **The greeting** | "Yung ninong sa Dubai, naka-video greeting sa screen habang kumakain kayo" | The OFW-family reality at every celebration |
| 📸 **The wall fills itself** | "200 photos galing sa guests — hindi mo hiningi, nag-scan lang sila" | The mom who knows the photographer can't be everywhere |

**Proof line for everything:** *"Totoong event 'to, hindi mockup"* — always
paired with real pool-party footage.

## 3. The engine — three loops, ranked

### Loop 1 — Supplier-led (the bet — 50% of weekly time)

Target list: kiddie party hosts / emcees, magicians-hosts, sound & lights,
event stylists, balloon/styling suppliers, photobooth operators — Laguna,
Batangas, Cavite first. They're all on Facebook, findable in 30 minutes via
"kiddie party host Laguna" + supplier groups.

The pitch to them is **status, then money**:
> Your program, but it looks like a concert production — trivia on the big
> screen, live leaderboard, ikaw pa rin ang may hawak ng mic.

The deal (simple, generous — we're buying distribution, not margin):
- **Free Reelday on their next 2 gigs** (their clients experience it free).
- After that: **₱500 referral per booked event**, or they bundle it into their
  package at whatever markup they want (we charge them ₱1,490, they charge the
  client ₱2,500+ — their margin, their incentive).
- We supply: a 1-page "how to run the trivia segment" script + the QR poster.

Weekly quota: **10 supplier DMs, 2 follow-ups each.** Close target: 3 active
partners by Jul 13.

### Loop 2 — The room (every event harvests the next — 20% of time)

At every event we run or a partner runs:
1. **The wall pitches itself**: last slide / idle rotation includes one tile —
   *"Gusto mo 'to sa party mo? reelday.ph — scan to see your own demo."*
   (Product tweak, small: an optional promo tile on the wall rotation for
   launch-period events. Until built, the printed QR poster near the screen
   does the same job.)
2. **Capture 60 seconds of phone footage**: kids seeing themselves on screen,
   the trivia tally racing, the winner moment. Raw > polished.
3. **Same-week follow-up**: ask the host for the testimonial + "sino sa guests
   mo ang may party soon?" Referral ask while the high is fresh.

### Loop 3 — Documentary content (proof layer — 30% of time)

Social's job is NOT discovery — it's making the brand undeniable when a mom or
a host checks the profile after a DM. Style decision: **document, don't
advertise.** Raw phone footage, founder voice, Taglish captions, real names
(with permission). No more polished brand reels until there's revenue.

**One series, owned:** 🎈 ***"Ang nangyari sa party ni ___"*** — every real
event becomes one episode: 20–40s, climax first (REVIEW-LOGIC.md rules:
payoff in first 3s, context by 8s, end card last 4s), one CTA.

Cadence: **3 posts/week** (Mon-Wed-Sat evenings), IG first (best historical
reach), same cut to FB Reels + TikTok at zero extra cost. Stories: countdown +
behind-the-scenes, 5 min/day.

Standing CTA everywhere: **"Comment PARTY or DM us — sasagutin ka namin with a
30-second demo link."**

## 4. Seven weeks (Jun 11 → Jul 31)

**Week 1 · Jun 11–15 — Re-aim.**
Flip all 3 bios + pinned posts to the interactive positioning & launch offer.
Create the `LAUNCH1490` coupon. Cut pool-party episode #1 + the trivia-moment
clip. Build the supplier hit list (30 names, Laguna-first). Send the first 10
supplier DMs. *Exit: positioning live, 2 posts up, 10 suppliers contacted.*

**Week 2 · Jun 16–22 — Supplier sprint.**
10 more supplier DMs + follow-ups. Goal: book **2 supplier-demo events** (their
gigs, free for their client). Start parent DMs (5/day — moms posting binyag /
1st-birthday prep in groups). Episode #2 up. *Exit: 2 partners verbally in, 1
demo event scheduled.*

**Week 3 · Jun 23–29 — First money.**
Run/support the first partner demo event → harvest the room (Loop 2). Turn on
ads (see §5) with the best pool-party clip. Push every warm parent lead to
book at launch price. *Exit: first 1–3 paid bookings, footage from a second
real event.*

**Weeks 4–5 · Jun 30–Jul 13 — Compound.**
One partner event per week minimum. Episode per event. Scale the ad that
produces conversations, kill the rest. Second supplier wave (next 20 names,
widen to Cavite/Batangas). Mid-point scoreboard review Jul 6. *Exit: 5+
cumulative paid, 3 active partners, 3 testimonials banked.*

**Weeks 6–7 · Jul 14–31 — Deadline close.**
"Launch price ends July 31" becomes every caption, every Story, every
follow-up. Personal message to every lead ever logged: one ask, one deadline.
Boost the countdown post (reserved ₱1.5k). Jul 31: close it for real — the
price goes to ₱2,990 on Aug 1 (credibility = actually raising it). *Exit:
8–12 cumulative paid events, supplier engine running without daily pushing.*

## 6. Scripts

**Supplier DM (host/emcee — the most important script in this plan):**
> Hi [name]! Napanood ko 'yung hosting niyo sa [event] — solid! 🎤 Quick one:
> I built **reelday.ph**, an interactive wall for parties. Para sa host, ito
> 'yung program upgrade: trivia questions about the celebrant sa big screen,
> guests sumasagot sa phones nila, live leaderboard, winner reveal with
> fireworks — ikaw pa rin ang star, pero parang concert na 'yung production.
> Gusto ko sana i-offer: **libre siya sa next 2 gigs mo** para makita mo kung
> paano siya tumama sa crowd mo. Pag gusto mo siya, may referral fee or
> pwede mo i-bundle sa package mo at ikaw bahala sa presyo. G ka ba sa
> 10-minute call or demo link muna?

**Parent DM (mom with upcoming binyag/birthday, found via groups):**
> Hi [name]! Nakita ko pong naghahanda kayo para sa [event] ni [child] — ang
> exciting! 🎉 Ako 'yung gumawa ng **reelday.ph** — interactive party wall:
> photos ng guests lumalabas live sa TV/screen niyo pag nag-scan sila ng QR,
> tapos may **trivia game tungkol kay [child]** na sasagutan ng guests sa
> phones nila — may leaderboard at winner! May video greetings din para sa
> mga hindi makakapunta. Launch price po namin ngayon: **₱1,490** (regular
> ₱2,990), with money-back guarantee kung hindi mapuno ang wall. Gusto niyo
> po ng 30-second demo link para makita niyo mismo?

**Group thread reply (helpful-member mode — never a pitch post):**
> Sa program po — eto 'yung pinaka-hit sa party na pinuntahan namin recently:
> live wall sa TV kung saan lumalabas agad 'yung photos ng guests, tapos may
> trivia game about the celebrant na sinasagot nila sa phone, may live
> leaderboard pa 😄 Hindi na umupo 'yung mga tita. Happy to share kung paano,
> message niyo lang ako!

**Objection bank:**
- *"May WiFi ba kailangan?"* → "Venue WiFi or isang mobile hotspot lang po —
  may setup guide kami at tinetest natin bago ang event."
- *"Baka walang mag-participate."* → "Busog Guarantee po: kung hindi napuno ng
  guests ang wall, full refund. Pero promise, pag lumabas 'yung unang photo
  sa screen, sunod-sunod na."
- *"Mahal ba?"* → "₱1,490 one-time para sa buong event — mas mura pa sa
  photobooth, at kasama na 'yung games, greetings, at event website."

or run real events.** Views are weather.

### Kill / scale rules (pre-committed so future-you doesn't negotiate)
- **Jul 6, fewer than 2 active partners** → suppliers aren't biting on free;
  switch the supplier offer to straight wholesale (₱990/event, they resell) and
  shift 2 weeks of supplier time into the parent ad + DM lane.
- **Jul 6, zero paid bookings** → the offer is the problem, not the channel:
  test "first 10 events ₱990" for one week before touching anything else.
- **Any channel produces a booking** → triple its time allocation the next
  week. **Any channel at zero conversations after 2 honest weeks** → drop it.
- TikTok is a free re-post bin. The moment it costs extra minutes, it's cut.

## 8. The week, on a job-search schedule

- **Lunch (10 min, phone):** reply to every DM/comment; log leads.
- **Mon/Wed/Sat evening (60 min):** publish the episode → then 5 supplier DMs
  + 5 parent DMs + 2 group replies.
- **Tue/Thu evening (45 min):** follow-ups (suppliers first), ad-chat replies,
  next episode rough cut.
- **Weekend block (2–3 h):** attend/support the week's partner event (Loop 2)
  or batch-produce content; Sunday scoreboard.

*Total: ~7–9 h/week. If a week collapses (interviews, life): do ONLY the
supplier DMs and the follow-ups. The engine is Loop 1; everything else is
paint.*
