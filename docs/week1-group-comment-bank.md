# Week 1 — Group Comment Bank (rotate these)

Copy-paste replies for the common thread types in PH wedding/debut/event
groups, plus 3 reworded soft-posts. **Rotate and tweak** — pasting identical
text across groups is the #1 spam trigger. Lead helpful, mention the wall
naturally, then move interested people to DM.

> Pattern for all of these: be genuinely useful first → soft mention → "DM me
> and I'll share" (never a hard sell in the comment).

---

## A. Comment replies (Mode 1 — reply on OTHER people's threads, no approval needed)

**1. "Any unique/different reception or program ideas?"**
> One that always gets a reaction lately: a **live photo wall** — guests scan a
> QR and their phone photos/videos pop up on the venue screen in real time. Turns
> the whole crowd into your second photographers 📸 Super interactive. Happy to
> share how it works if you want 🙂

**2. "Looking for affordable suppliers / budget-friendly tips"**
> Budget tip: instead of a 2nd photographer, a **QR photo wall** lets your guests'
> own phones fill your gallery — you end up with hundreds of candid shots for a
> fraction of the cost. Pwede ko i-share details kung interested ka 💛

**3. "How do I make guests feel more involved / entertained?"**
> Guests love feeling part of the show. A live photo wall + simple on-screen polls
> ("best dressed?", trivia about the couple) keeps everyone engaged — their
> uploads and votes appear live on the big screen. Ping me if you want to see a
> sample 🙂

**4. "Photographer / videographer recommendations?"** *(complement, don't compete)*
> Definitely book a pro for the key shots — but a nice add-on is a **guest photo
> wall** so you also capture the candid angles the photographer can't be
> everywhere for (guests' phones cover the rest, live on the screen). Happy to
> explain if helpful.

**5. "What's a must-have / something you regret NOT having?"**
> A lot of couples say they wish they'd collected guests' photos on the night
> itself — so many great candids just stay stuck in people's phones. A live photo
> wall fixes exactly that (QR → shows on the venue screen → you keep them all
> after). Glad to share more 🤍

**6. "Debut ideas — something different for my daughter's 18th?"**
> For an 18th, an interactive **photo wall** is a hit with the barkada — they scan
> a QR and their pics/videos show up live on the screen all night, plus you can
> run fun polls. Very "their generation." I can send a quick sample if you'd like
> 🙂

**7. "Photo booth recommendations?"** *(adjacent — position as the modern, no-line option)*
> Photo booths are fun! A newer twist some are doing instead is a **live photo
> wall** — no line, no printer: every guest's phone becomes the booth, and shots
> appear on the big screen instantly. Pwede ko ipakita how it looks kung curious
> ka.

**8. "How do we share photos with everyone after the event?"**
> Easiest way: a QR photo wall — guests upload during the event, it shows live on
> screen, and everything's saved in one gallery you can share after. No more
> chasing people for their photos 🙂 Happy to walk you through it.

## C. Usage notes
- **Rotate** — don't reuse the same line twice in one day across groups.
- **Personalize** — reference the poster's actual event/date when you can.
- **One soft-post per group max**, only where rules allow / on promo days.
- **Move to DM fast** — once someone replies "interested", switch to the warm-DM
  script (`week1-outreach-pack.md`) + send the Jotform application link for hero
  events, or the FOUNDING20 link for paid.
- **Log everyone** in the lead sheet (Channel = FB Group, note which group).
- Build a few helpful comments in a group **before** dropping a soft-post — admins
  approve active members faster than brand-new accounts posting an ad.
